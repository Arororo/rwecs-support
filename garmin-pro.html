Companion app

Download on the App Store

Setup steps

  1. Install and open the RWECS companion app on your phone.
  2. Sign in and buy the Pro subscription in the companion app.
  3. Keep Garmin Connect and RWECS app active in background.
  4. On your watch: RWECS → Subscription → Sync with phone.
  5. Wait for sync to complete; status should update in pre-session screen.

Pro benefits

  • Custom LOC alert threshold (Free uses the built-in default threshold).
  • Activity type selection (Run / Walk).
  • Force tap split gesture on workout screens (same action as pressing Back for split).
  • RWECS metrics written into your saved activity so they appear in Garmin Connect (charts and activity details).
  • Additional workout metric views during the session.
Required

What must be ready

  • Garmin watch paired with your phone in Garmin Connect.
  • Bluetooth enabled and phone near your watch.
  • RWECS companion app installed on your phone.
  • Active Pro subscription in the companion app.

Troubleshooting

  • If watch says Sync failed, open Garmin Connect + RWECS app on phone and retry.
  • If no phone is connected, connect/pair first, then run sync again.
  • If subscription still shows Free, verify subscription status in the RWECS app.

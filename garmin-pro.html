RWECS Premium for Garmin

Set up Premium on your Garmin watch

Use this page to understand what Premium unlocks and how to activate sync between your watch and phone.

If you opened this from the watch, return to Premium → Sync with phone after setup.

Premium benefits

  • Access to full session history (not only recent workouts).
  • Detailed charts for recorded session data.
  • Advanced dual-sensor workflow as features roll out.
Required

What must be ready

  • Garmin watch paired with your phone in Garmin Connect.
  • Bluetooth enabled and phone near your watch.
  • RWECS companion app installed on your phone.
  • Active Premium subscription in the companion app.

Setup steps

  1. Install and open the RWECS companion app on your phone.
  2. Sign in and purchase/restore Premium in the app.
  3. Keep Garmin Connect and RWECS app active in background.
  4. On your watch: RWECS → Premium → Sync with phone.
  5. Wait for sync to complete; status should update in pre-session screen.

Companion app

Install the mobile app first. Premium is managed there.

Download on the App Store

Troubleshooting

  • If watch says Sync failed, open Garmin Connect + RWECS app on phone and retry.
  • If no phone is connected, connect/pair first, then run sync again.
  • If Premium still shows Free, verify subscription status in the RWECS app.
